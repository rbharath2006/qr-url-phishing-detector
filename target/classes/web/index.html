[ HUMAN FIREWALL / AWARENESS MODE ]

Phishing doesn't always look dangerous.

QR phishing — sometimes called quishing — hides malicious destinations behind something that looks harmless.

01FAKE LOGIN PAGES

A QR code can send you to a cloned banking, email, payment or social-media login page.

DEFENSE → Check the domain before entering credentials.
02PAYMENT TRAPS

Attackers may replace legitimate QR stickers with their own to redirect payments.

DEFENSE → Verify the recipient and amount inside your payment app.
03SHORTENED LINKS

Short URLs hide the final destination and make it harder to recognize a malicious domain.

DEFENSE → Expand or inspect shortened links before trusting them.
04URGENCY & FEAR

Messages such as “account blocked” or “verify in 5 minutes” are designed to rush your judgment.

DEFENSE → Pause. Verify using the organization's official app or website.

THE 7-SECOND PHISHING CHECK

01Stop before scanning unknown codes.
02Read the complete domain carefully.
03Watch for misspellings and lookalike domains.
04Be suspicious of unexpected urgency.
05Never share OTPs or passwords through a QR prompt.
06Verify payments and recipients independently.
07If something feels wrong, close it.
